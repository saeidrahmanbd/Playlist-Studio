## 🚀 Start here — 3 easy steps

| Step | What to do |
|:---:|---|
| **1️⃣** | **[⬇️ Download Playlist Studio](https://github.com/saeidrahmanbd/Playlist-Studio/releases/download/v5.0.0/Playlist-Studio-5.0.exe)** |
| **2️⃣** | When the download finishes, double-click `Playlist-Studio-5.0.exe`. **No installer, Python or separate VLC installation is needed.** |
| **3️⃣** | Open your M3U/M3U8 playlist, make your changes, and click **Save**. |

> ✅ **That's all you need to get started.** The first launch may take a few seconds.

> 🔒 **Download safely:** get the app only from the official release linked above.

## ✨ What can I do with it?

| 📂 Open & Import | ✏️ Edit | 🔎 Test & Play | 🔌 Connect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open a playlist from your computer | Add, rename, sort, move and delete channels or categories | Scan streams and export results to Excel | Compatible Xtream Codes services |
| Drag and drop a file | Edit channel names, numbers, logos, stream URLs and EPG information | Play channels in the built-in player | Compatible Stalker Portal / MAC services |
